Sisben Consulta Puntaje: Check Your Group, Fix Mistakes, and Use the Result Correctly

Many Colombians type sisben consulta puntaje into a phone and then stare at a result they do not trust. A teacher in Barranquilla checked three times in one week and got the same letter, yet she did not know what to do next. This guide gives that next step clearly.

Start With the Right Page, Not a Random Search

Search results mix official pages, news stories, and copycat sites. Use the official Sisbén group consultation page on sisben.gov.co. That page asks for document type, then the number, then a verification step. It does not ask for payment or bank details. If a page asks for money to show your group, close it at once.

Before you type, read the number from the card itself, not from memory. A transposed digit is the most frequent cause of a blank result. Type slowly, check each digit, and only then submit. The page should display your group, consultation date, and record tied to your document. Save that screen.

How Groups Are Built From Your Survey

Sisbén IV does not grade people. It groups households by living conditions, using answers from a home survey. The survey records housing, water and electricity access, sewerage, garbage collection, household size, schooling, and income capacity. No single answer decides the group alone.

The four groups run from A to D. Group A, A1 to A5, means extreme poverty. Group B, B1 to B7, means moderate poverty. Group C, C1 to C18, means vulnerable. Group D, D1 to D21, means neither poor nor vulnerable. A subgroup near the start, such as A2, sits closer to the difficult end. A subgroup near the end, such as D20, sits closer to the next group.

Common Checking Mistakes That Hide Your Result

A vendor in a Bogotá market once told me his result vanished after he renewed his identity card. He had entered the old number, not the new one, so the system found no match. Always check with the document you hold today, not an old copy.

Another mistake is checking too soon after a survey. A team may visit on Monday, yet the record may need days of review before it appears in the national base. Wait a few days, then check again with the same number. If the page still shows nothing, visit a local office and ask staff to confirm whether the record was received.

A third mistake is mixing two results. Some people compare a program portal result with a Sisbén page result and assume one must be wrong. Programs can apply their own cutoffs after Sisbén, so a program decision and a Sisbén group are not the same thing.

RUI and Sisbén Side by Side

On 1 August 2026, the Registro Universal de Ingresos, called RUI, began operating as a new instrument for targeting social spending. Many readers assumed Sisbén had closed. It had not closed. Sisbén remains in force for consultation, surveys, and updates.

A RUI consultation during transition can display two separate lines: the new RUI group, and the Sisbén IV group recorded on 31 July 2026. Do not merge them. When a form asks for Sisbén, write the Sisbén line. When it asks for RUI, write the RUI line. If it names neither, ask the office which line it needs.

Certificate, Updates, and Help by Phone

Once your group is visible, download the affiliation certificate and keep a printed copy with your household papers. Check that the name, document number, group, and date are readable. Print again if any line is unclear.

Update your record when your home changes. A new baby, a move, a member leaving, or a change in work can all matter. Use the Sisbén Citizen Portal after registering and confirming your email, or go to a local office. For a fresh survey, the household head must be an adult and present on visit day, and each member needs a valid document.

For phone help, contact the Departamento Nacional de Planeación, known as DNP. In Bogotá, dial 195 or (601) 3815000 and choose option 2. Outside Bogotá, dial 01 8000 121221.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I check my sisben consulta puntaje from a phone without visiting an office?

Yes. Open the official consultation page in your phone browser, choose your document type, enter the number exactly as printed, and finish the check on screen. The page shows your group and consultation date. Save a screenshot. An office visit is only needed if the record is missing or must be updated.

What should I do if my group looks wrong after a recent survey?

Wait a few days first, because a new record needs time for review. Then check again with your current document number and confirm every digit. If the result still looks wrong, take your document to an office and ask them to check if the record arrived, and whether a correction or a fresh survey is needed.

Does a Sisbén group alone guarantee a subsidy or program place?

No. A group is only a classification. Each program sets its own cutoff, required documents, deadlines, and availability. A group in A or B can help a program target you, but approval still depends on that program's own rules and checks. Never assume a place from the group alone.

How often should I check my group after my household changes?

Check right after a major change, such as a move, a birth, a death, a member leaving, or new work or schooling. Also check before you apply to a program, so it sees current facts. If nothing has changed, there is no need to check every day.

Where can I get help if the consultation page will not load?

Try again later, check your internet connection, and confirm the document type and number. A RUI consultation can also show the Sisbén IV group as a separate line. If the page still will not load, call DNP in Bogotá on 195 or (601) 3815000, option 2, or dial 01 8000 121221 from the rest of the country, or visit an office with your document.

Use the Result, Then Move On

Check the group, save the certificate, update the record when life changes, and keep the consultation date in your notes. That routine prevents delays. A group is a starting point for programs, not a final decision.
